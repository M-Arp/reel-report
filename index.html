Statistics and patterns from your IMDb ratings.

Drop your ratings file here

Use the .csv file IMDb gives you when you export your ratings. It's read in this browser tab and never uploaded.

Getting your ratings file from IMDb

  1. Sign in to IMDb and open Your ratings from the menu under your name.
  2. Click the ⋯ menu at the top of the list and choose Export.
  3. IMDb builds the file in a minute or two and lists it on its Exports page. Download it from there.
  4. Drop that file on the box here.

    Likes and dislikes

    Your favourites, your least favourites, and where you part ways with the IMDb crowd. Crowd comparisons only use titles with at least 1,000 votes.

    Actors and franchises

    IMDb's export doesn't include cast or series information. Reel Report can look each title up on TMDB, a free movie database, to fill that in. You need a free TMDB API key: create an account, then go to Settings → API and copy the API key or the API Read Access Token.

    When you rated

    Titles rated each year, from the dates in your export.

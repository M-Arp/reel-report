Statistics and patterns from your IMDb ratings.

Drop your ratings file here

Use the .csv file IMDb gives you when you export your ratings. It's read in this browser tab and never uploaded.

Getting your ratings file from IMDb

  1. Sign in to IMDb and open Your ratings from the menu under your name.
  2. Click the ⋯ menu at the top of the list and choose Export.
  3. IMDb builds the file in a minute or two and lists it on its Exports page. Download it from there.
  4. Drop that file on the box here.
Example dataA made-up set of 190 ratings. Load your own file to replace it.

Likes and dislikes

Your favourites, your least favourites, and where you part ways with the IMDb crowd. Crowd comparisons only use titles with at least 1,000 votes.

Actors, franchises and studios

IMDb's export doesn't include cast, studio or network details, so Reel Report looks each title up on TMDB, a free movie database. This starts by itself when you load your file. Only the IMDb title IDs are sent; your ratings stay in your browser.

Films to watch next

Picked from TMDB's suggestions for the titles you rated highest, weighted toward genres you rate above your average and toward well-reviewed titles. Anything you've rated is left out.

Reel Report isn't affiliated with IMDb. Your ratings file stays in your browser. TMDB lookups send only IMDb title IDs to Reel Report's lookup service, which asks TMDB.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
